Product policy

Licensing overview

Effective 14 November 2026

Spotivo licensing is intended to connect authorized controller access with registered player devices. The exact licence quantity, duration, permitted users, screens, and renewal rules are stated in the customer’s accepted quotation or licence agreement.

Screen licences

Plans are generally calculated using active managed displays. A licence does not transfer ownership of the Spotivo software and may not be copied, resold, shared outside the licensed organization, reverse engineered, or used to bypass device and account restrictions.

Controller access

Controller credentials must be kept confidential and used only by authorized personnel. Account or device access may be restricted when misuse, credential sharing, cloning, tampering, or an expired or revoked licence is detected.

Player devices and offline use

A registered player may continue an already downloaded playlist during a temporary outage. Offline playback does not remove licence obligations. The player must reconnect as required to receive new assignments, report activity, and revalidate its authorization.

Changes and revocation

Administrators may replace or revoke registered devices under the applicable plan. Abuse, non-payment, unauthorized modification, or a material breach may result in suspension or revocation as permitted by the customer agreement.
