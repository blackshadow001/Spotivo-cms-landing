Ask another question ↗
What happens when the internet goes offline?+

After the assigned playlist and media have downloaded, the player can continue using its local cache. It checks for current assignments again when the connection returns.

Can screens be installed vertically?+

Yes. Each screen can be configured for portrait or landscape installation, with an additional 180° flip option when the physical display is mounted upside down.

Can video audio be turned off per screen?+

Yes. Screen audio is controlled from the control panel, allowing a display to play video sound or remain muted.

How are playlists assigned?+

A playlist can be sent to an individual screen, a screen group, or a location. This lets teams reuse content while keeping each destination under control.

Can I check whether an advertisement played?+

Playback reporting provides screen-specific activity, completed plays, errors, and timing information when the player has reported it.
