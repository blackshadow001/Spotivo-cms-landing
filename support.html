Before contacting support

  • Confirm the controller or backend service is reachable.
  • Check the TV date, time, Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection.
  • Record the exact error message and when it appeared.
  • For assignment issues, confirm the screen remains paired and assigned to the intended playlist.
