Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains how information submitted through the Spotivo commercial website is handled. It applies to this website and its enquiry form; a customer agreement may contain additional terms for the Spotivo controller and player services.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we may receive your name, work email, company, estimated number of screens, and the requirements you enter. Please do not submit passwords, payment information, or confidential media through the enquiry form.

How we use information

  • Respond to product, pricing, deployment, and support enquiries.
  • Prepare a requested demonstration or commercial quotation.
  • Protect the website and diagnose delivery problems.
  • Maintain records required for an ongoing business relationship or applicable law.

Form delivery provider

The enquiry form is delivered using FormSubmit. Information entered into the form is transmitted to that provider so it can be delivered to enterprise@spotivo.cloud. If you do not want to use the form, contact us directly by email.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell enquiry information. Information may be handled by service providers needed to operate the website, deliver enquiries, provide hosting, or comply with lawful requests.

Retention and security

Enquiries are retained only as long as reasonably required to respond, maintain a business record, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. No internet transmission is completely secure; avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete enquiry information, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. Contact enterprise@spotivo.cloud.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website and service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
